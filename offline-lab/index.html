What these results mean

Pass/fail covers explicit text assertions only. Evidence coverage is English token overlap, not semantic faithfulness. Unsupported words and numbers are review cues, not proven hallucinations. Negation, paraphrases and factual contradictions need human review. This app does not generate LLM responses.
